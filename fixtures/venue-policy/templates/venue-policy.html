Section I. Coverage C: Medical Payments

1. Insuring agreement

We will pay medical expenses as described below for bodily injury caused by an accident on the premises shown in the Declarations, provided that the accident takes place during the policy period.

The expenses must be incurred and reported to us within 365 days of the date of the accident.

The injured person must submit to examination, at our expense, by physicians of our choice as often as we reasonably require.

We will make these payments regardless of fault.

2. What we pay

We will pay reasonable expenses for first aid administered at the time of an accident, and for necessary medical, surgical, X-ray and dental services, including prosthetic devices.

We pay each service at no more than the amount the Schedule of Medical Benefits allows for it per unit. A service the Schedule does not list is paid only after our review.

The each person limit is the most we will pay for all medical expenses because of bodily injury sustained by any one person. The medical payments aggregate limit is the most we will pay for all persons during the policy period.

3. Minimum claim

We will not pay a medical payments claim of less than $25.00.

Section I. Coverage P: Damage to Premises and Property

1. Insuring agreement

We will pay for direct physical loss of or damage to the premises shown in the Declarations, and to the equipment, fixtures and furnishings you own or rent there, caused by an accident or by the act of any person attending an event at the premises during the policy period.

2. Deductible

We will pay only the amount of loss in any one occurrence that is more than the deductible each occurrence shown in the Declarations, up to the each occurrence limit.

The property aggregate limit is the most we will pay for all loss under Coverage P during the policy period.

3. Valuation

We will pay the cost to repair the damaged property, or to replace it with property of like kind and quality, whichever is less.

Section II. Exclusions

This insurance does not apply to:

  1. Employees and volunteers. Bodily injury to any employee or volunteer worker of the insured, arising out of and in the course of their work for the insured.
  2. Injury before the event. Bodily injury that began before the event, or a condition the injured person had before they arrived at the premises.
  3. Injury away from the premises. Bodily injury that takes place away from the premises shown in the Declarations.
  4. Care, custody or control. Damage to personal property in the care, custody or control of the insured, including property left with the insured for safekeeping.

Section III. Conditions

1. Duties in the event of an accident, occurrence or claim

You must see to it that we are notified as soon as practicable of an accident or occurrence that may result in a claim, at the address shown in the Declarations. The notice should include how, when and where it took place, and the names of any injured persons and witnesses.

You must send us copies of any demands, bills, notices or records received in connection with the claim, and cooperate with us in the investigation and settlement of the claim.

2. Other insurance

Coverage C Medical Payments is excess over any other insurance available to the injured person, such as their own health plan. That insurance pays first, and we will pay only the amount it leaves the injured person to pay, up to the medical expenses this policy covers.

3. Transfer of rights of recovery against others to us

If the insured has rights to recover all or part of any payment we have made under this policy, those rights are transferred to us. The insured must do nothing after loss to impair them. At our request, the insured will bring suit or transfer those rights to us and help us enforce them.

This condition does not apply to Coverage C Medical Payments.

4. Legal action against us

No person or organization has a right to sue us under this policy unless all of its terms have been fully complied with.

5. Cancellation

The first named insured shown in the Declarations may cancel this policy by mailing or delivering to us advance written notice of cancellation.
