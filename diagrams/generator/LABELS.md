# Diagram label standard

Label standard for every box and arrow (kept in sync with diagrams/generator/LABELS.md):

1. A screen is written as: page name (route), with the real route or tab from web/src, for example organizer portal (/organizer, Claims), tap page (/t/code), recap (/t/code), photo page (/photo).
2. A person is one word: attendee, medic, organizer, photographer, staff.
3. A code piece is its exact name only: watch_bands, buy_merch, my_wallet.
4. A service shows its logo. A wordmark logo (one that already says the name, such as Elastic, Capital One, Vultr or Bright Data) replaces the product name. An icon-only logo keeps the product name beside it. The role goes in brackets under the logo, at most two words, for example (Evidence Engine) or (wallet).
5. A device is its short name: band, station marker, exit marker, gateway, venue laptop, security camera.
6. An arrow label is a verb of 1 to 3 words, or nothing: opens incident, deposits, tags photo, files claim.
7. No sentences, no "the", no "in the", no possessives, no explanation in a box.

8. Frame titles are one name, never a sentence: BandIt (the overview), Architecture, SpacetimeDB, Vision, Event data, Evidence Engine, Decision Engine, Claude, Social, Merch and wallet, Capital One Nessie, Vultr, Hardware.
9. Make every frame visual: where an icon, a logo or a small picture can replace words on a box, use it. Text stays only for names and 1 to 3 word arrow verbs.
10. No placeholders: no TBD, TODO, "planned", "not built" or "?", and no box for a part that is not built.
11. No subtitle, caption or note under a frame title. The picture explains the frame.

Logos live in diagrams/logos/. Use the word list above for every label, and no sponsor track or prize names on the diagrams.

Terms: use the word list in var/devpost/TERMS.md (one word for one thing).
