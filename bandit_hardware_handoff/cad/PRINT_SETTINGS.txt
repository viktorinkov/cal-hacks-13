BandIt v2 wristband puck: print files and settings
====================================================

Files (mm, already oriented for the bed, no rotation needed)
  bandit_v2_base.stl             case body, prints upright as is. 64 x 42 x 13.6 mm, ~13 g
  bandit_v2_lid_facedown.stl     lid, already flipped so the flat top is on the bed. 62 x 40 x 1.2 mm, ~3 g
  bandit_v2_button_facedown.stl  optional button cap, already flipped. 5.8 mm dia x 1.6 mm, ~0.1 g

Colours
  base + button: black PLA
  lid: translucent or white PLA (or translucent PETG). The lid has a 0.6 mm thin ring over the
       LEDs; white PLA glows through it fine, clear/natural glows brighter.

Slicer settings (PrusaSlicer / Prusa CORE One or MK4 profile, 0.4 mm nozzle)
  Layer height          0.20 mm   (0.20mm STRUCTURAL or BALANCED profile)
  Perimeters            3
  Top / bottom layers   profile default (5 / 4)
  Infill                15 %, gyroid
  Supports              NONE  (the only overhangs are the 17.5 mm strap channel roof and the
                               10.6 mm USB window top, both designed as bridges)
  Brim                  none;  skirt is fine
  Elephant foot comp.   profile default (0.2 mm). Do not disable: the lid relies on it.
  Seam                  anywhere / nearest
  Material              PLA, 215 C nozzle / 60 C bed (Prusament PLA profile defaults)

Orientation check in the slicer
  base:   open side up, two rails on the bed, USB window in one end wall
  lid:    the big flat face on the bed, the thin ring and the 6.5 mm hole visible from above
  button: the small 5.2 mm face on the bed, 5.8 mm step above it

Multiple per bed
  Core One (250 x 220): 15 bases + 15 buttons fit as a 5 x 3 grid with the parts turned 90 degrees,
  4 mm apart, 5 mm from the edge; 15 lids the same way. bandit_v2_bed_black.stl and
  bandit_v2_bed_clear.stl in the zip are those plates ready to slice.

After printing
  Ring should drop into the 38.3 mm pocket with slight play; battery into the 37 x 32 pocket;
  the XIAO's end passes between the two posts (18.4 mm gap). Lid sits in the 0.8 mm rebate with
  0.2 mm clearance per side. If the lid is tight, scrape the first-layer edge with a blade.
